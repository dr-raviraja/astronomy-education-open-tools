# Astronomy Education Open Tools (Low-Cost & Bilingual OER)

Open-source, tactile astronomy lab templates, printable SVG/PDF generators, and bilingual (English & Telugu) educator guides developed by **Dr. Ravi Raja Pothineni** through **The Astronomical Society for Training, Research and Outreach (ASTRO)** and **Novae Astro Club**.

Designed for resource-constrained classrooms, rural schools, and public sky-watching sessions where commercial telescopes or digital planetariums are unavailable.

## Modules Included
1. **`sundial_17_5N/`** – Python SVG generator for a horizontal sundial calibrated to Hyderabad's latitude ($\phi = 17.5^\circ\text{ N}$), including gnomon geometry and equation-of-time notes.
2. **`spectroscope_cardboard/`** – Step-by-step construction guide and emission/absorption line lab using recycled cardboard and a CD/DVD diffraction grating.
3. **`crater_impact_lab/`** – Tactile lunar cratering worksheet (sand + limestone powder trays) exploring projectile energy, ejecta rays, and central peaks.
4. **`solar_rotation_tracker/`** – Safe projection/solar-filter sunspot tracking log for measuring the Sun's synodic rotation period (~27 days).
